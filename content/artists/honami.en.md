Born in 1991 on a small island in the Seto Inland Sea, Ehime. _A Bite of
Bone_ (2021) took the Grand Prize for Short Animation at the 45th Ottawa
International Animation Festival, along with 28 further awards. Her latest
film _Eri_ (2026) is an Official Selection of the 58th Directors' Fortnight
at Cannes. She also researches sexuality and gender in animation.
