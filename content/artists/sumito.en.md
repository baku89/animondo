Born in 1980 in Tokachi, Hokkaido; based in the northern foothills of
Nagano. He moved to the UK at fifteen and graduated from the Royal College
of Art in 2004. His recent installations and paintings project the same
figure many times over within a single space; _Iizuna Fair_ is on permanent
display at the Nagano Prefectural Museum of Art.
