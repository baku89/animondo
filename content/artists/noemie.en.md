Trained in illustration at Saint-Luc in Brussels, Noémie Marsily is a multidisciplinary author and artist. She has published several comics including Fétiche (2013) with Les Requins Marteaux, and Memet (2019) with l'Employé du Moi. Alongside Carl Roosens, she co-directs animated short films including Poodle (2010), Around the Lake (2013), and I don't feel anything anymore (2016). Her artistic practice has evolved over several years, through meetings and projects, between comics, illustration and animated cinema.
