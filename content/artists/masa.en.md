Working on the themes of time, space and memory, she animates frame by
frame with drawing, 8mm film and cyanotype. Her films have screened at
Animafest Zagreb, GLAS, Tampere and Image Forum Festival, and she took an
Honorable Mention in New Cosmos of Photography 2021. Since September 2025
she has been enrolled in the animation MA at the Estonian Academy of Arts.
